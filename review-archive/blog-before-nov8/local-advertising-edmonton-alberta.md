# How to run local advertising that brings better enquiries in Edmonton

Local advertising works best when the targeting, message, landing page, and follow-up all describe the same service. Choose a specific buying situation, reach the people you can genuinely serve, and judge results by qualified enquiries and sales rather than cheap clicks.

A small Edmonton business does not need a national media plan to start. It needs a test that answers a useful question: can this channel bring suitable opportunities at a cost the business can support?

KR8TIV · kr8tiv.io connects creative work with that question. The studio designs visual experiences and helps businesses find customers through clearer brands, websites, and campaigns. This guide shows how to plan a local advertising test without treating a budget as a guarantee.

## Should you use Google Search ads or paid social?

Use search when people already look for the service you sell. Use paid social when you need to demonstrate the service, introduce an offer, or reach suitable people before they actively search.

Imagine a fictional Edmonton mobile detailing business. A person searching for interior detailing may already want to book. A short social video could help someone understand an unfamiliar service or see how the appointment works. Those are different buying situations, so the creative and expectations should differ.

Search is not automatically profitable because the intent is clear. Competition, enquiry quality, price, and capacity affect the result. Social is not automatically cheap because an impression costs less. You still need suitable buyers to act.

Start with one channel if the budget cannot support a meaningful test on both. Choose based on your buyer's behaviour and the evidence you can show, not the platform you enjoy using personally.

## How much should an Alberta small business spend on ads?

Set a test budget from the question you need answered, the likely cost of reaching buyers, and the loss you can tolerate. Separate media spend from creative production, setup, management, and landing-page work.

Here is an illustrative search scenario, not a market benchmark. At an assumed CAD $5 per click, CAD $600 in media buys about 120 clicks. At an assumed 5% click-to-enquiry rate, that produces six enquiries. If half qualify, you have three qualified opportunities before considering sales.

Every assumption can be wrong. Actual click costs and enquiry rates vary by service, competition, timing, and account setup. Three opportunities are also too few to establish a reliable long-term average.

Use this calculation before approving the test:

> Media budget ÷ assumed cost per click × assumed enquiry rate × assumed qualification rate = estimated qualified enquiries.

Then compare the estimate with your allowable acquisition cost and close rate. If even optimistic assumptions cannot support the economics, change the service, offer, or channel before spending.

Keep enough budget for the page and follow-up. Paying to send people into a broken booking flow is a repair problem, not an advertising strategy.

## How do you stop a local campaign reaching the wrong places?

Set locations deliberately and inspect both the targeting options and actual lead locations. A city name in the campaign title does not limit where every prospect lives.

Google's [advanced location guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en) distinguishes people in or regularly in a location from people who also show interest in it. The default can include that broader interest group.

For a service restricted to nearby properties, consider whether presence-based targeting better fits the business. A venue attracting visitors from elsewhere may have a reason to include interest. Check the options available for the campaign type you use rather than applying one setting everywhere.

Location signals are imperfect. Google says targeting is a best effort and does not guarantee complete accuracy. Ask for the service location in the enquiry flow, inspect results, and exclude unsuitable areas where appropriate.

Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, and other nearby communities are not interchangeable promises. Include them only if the business serves them. For onsite work, compare travel cost and job value before expanding across Alberta.

## What should a local search ad say?

Name the service, establish fit, and give an accurate next step. Match the ad to a page that delivers the same information.

For the fictional detailing business, a useful message might mention interior detailing in Edmonton, the vehicle types accepted, and how to request an appointment. Avoid "same-day appointments" unless the business can reliably provide them and keep the ad current.

Group related searches by the service and destination they need. Someone looking for fleet work may need different information from someone booking a personal vehicle. The distinction matters more than creating dozens of tiny groups without a clear reason.

Review the search terms available in the account. Exclude clearly irrelevant intent, such as jobs, training, or equipment sales when those are not your offer. Do not blindly exclude every research question. Some buyers investigate before they enquire.

Keep a note explaining why you add each important exclusion. A future account review should not have to guess whether a word blocks unwanted traffic or useful buyers.

## What makes paid-social creative useful rather than decorative?

Show a buying-relevant detail early. The viewer needs to understand what the service does before a brand animation uses up their attention.

A useful short video can show what is included, how access works, what preparation the customer handles, or how a quote is assessed. Use real footage with permission. Label concepts and demonstrations honestly.

For our example, one video could show the appointment handoff. Another could explain what an interior-detail package includes. Avoid implying every vehicle achieves the same result. The final condition depends on the vehicle and agreed scope.

Design for the placement. Check captions, cropping, readable type, and the action button on a phone. Music should not carry information the viewer needs to understand the offer.

Test a small number of meaningfully different ideas. Changing a background colour is unlikely to teach as much as comparing two real customer concerns. Keep the offer steady while you learn which concern draws suitable enquiries.

## What should happen after someone clicks?

Send the person to a page that confirms the service, explains the practical details, and offers a clear route to enquire. Do not make them restart their research on an unrelated homepage.

The fictional detailing page needs the service area, package boundaries, vehicle suitability, booking process, and any relevant access requirements. If the business works outdoors, explain genuine weather limitations rather than hiding them until after booking.

Use a form that helps assess fit without demanding unnecessary personal information. A service location, vehicle category, requested service, and contact method might be enough to begin. Explain what happens after submission and set a response expectation you can meet.

Test a completed enquiry on the final page. Make sure a success message appears and a human receives the details. A click on the submit button is not proof that the form delivered a lead.

## How do you track whether ads bring customers?

Track the stages between a click and paid work. Count genuine enquiries separately from page views, button clicks, and qualified opportunities.

Set up measurement with appropriate privacy and consent handling for your tools and audience. Avoid sending sensitive customer details through analytics URLs or event labels. If calls are recorded, obtain appropriate advice and permissions rather than treating recording as a default.

Maintain an enquiry log alongside platform reports. Record which leads qualify, which receive quotes, and which become customers. A platform may take credit for a conversion another platform also reports. Do not add both totals and call the result unique customers.

Compare media-only cost with full campaign cost. A campaign can look efficient in the dashboard while requiring more creative and administration than the margin supports.

## When should you change or stop an advertising test?

Fix broken delivery, tracking, and irrelevant traffic promptly. Make performance decisions against a pre-agreed review point, available evidence, and the business's ability to absorb the spend.

Define stop conditions before launch. Examples include a broken form, spending beyond the authorised budget, or repeated enquiries for an unavailable service. Those need action even when the sample is small.

Other decisions need patience and judgement. A low-volume service may take longer to generate enough qualified opportunities. Do not repeatedly change the audience and offer before you can understand either.

At each review, write down what happened, what you believe caused it, and the one change you want to test. Include the person answering enquiries. Their notes often explain why a dashboard's "lead" never became a useful conversation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is CAD $500 enough to test local advertising?

It may support a narrow media test, but the answer depends on click costs, service value, and the evidence you need. It is not a reliable universal monthly budget, and creative or setup costs are separate.

### Can ads guarantee customers in Edmonton?

No. Ads can buy distribution, not guaranteed sales. Offer quality, competition, targeting, website experience, response, and delivery capacity all affect results.

### Do I need video to advertise a local business?

No. A strong still image or a clear search ad may suit the task. Use video when showing the service helps buyers understand it, with captions and permission for the material.

### Should I advertise across Canada?

Only if you can serve that market and afford to test it. A remote service may have national reach. An onsite business needs a service area that supports profitable delivery.
