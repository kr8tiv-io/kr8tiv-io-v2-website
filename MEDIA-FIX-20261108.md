# Media follow-up — 8 November 2026

Branch: `content/nov8`. Local work only; no push, deploy or upload.

## Causes found

1. **Desktop interlude:** the original poster and MP4 were both valid. In a cold desktop capture, the shared lazy loader called `play()` while the video still had readyState 0 and videoWidth 0. The browser stopped presenting its native poster before a decoded frame was paintable. The mobile capture happened before playback and retained the poster. The oversized rectangular black spread-shadows and the media's baked-in black ground made that loading gap read as an empty black box. A separate poster alone fixed visibility but did not remove the baked-in matte; actual screenshot review caught that, despite the DOM assertions passing.
2. **Shane card:** the real paired artwork already existed. Its lazy image was below the browser's initial fetch threshold (about 4,288px down the desktop page), so a full-page screenshot recorded it before loading. After scrolling, it decoded at its real 1600px width and showed the correct original front/back.
3. **Article hero:** the hero rendered correctly, but its intrinsic/OG dimensions were incorrectly declared as 1024×576. The real posters are 1280×720.

## Fixes

- An independent, eagerly loaded transparent WebP always renders the real chrome wordmark. It does not depend on the video element's poster-paint state.
- Original supplied media remains unchanged. Derived WebP/VP9-alpha WebM assets remove the near-black backdrop, not the bright chrome artwork. The source poster's RGB values are retained; only its near-black matte becomes transparent. A lossless intermediate is preserved outside the public build.
- Video has no source until it is on screen and motion is permitted. Reduced motion, site motion-off and Save-Data retain the still. The still is hidden only after a decoded video frame is ready to paint. If VP9 or its alpha channel is unsupported, the still remains instead of showing a black movie panel.
- No mask, inset shadow, darkening filter, overlay or blend mode acts on the chrome. The soft outward feather is a separate blurred radial layer behind it; the rectangular frame shadows are gone.
- Business-card previews load eagerly. The gallery capture now also scrolls/decode-checks every image before a full-page capture, rather than trusting HTTP 200 or DOM presence.
- Corrected article hero/index intrinsic dimensions and Article OG dimensions to the actual 1280×720. Added the WebM MIME declaration for static hosting.

A pixel-level check specifically rejects the previous black-box condition; near-black matte fraction is zero in all six final crops, and bright chrome is present.

- The article hero's still and normal-motion video both render. Desktop video was confirmed muted, playing and decoded.
