# How we built Evolve Eco Blasting to get found in Alberta search

Evolve Eco Blasting is an Alberta mobile dustless-blasting company. KR8TIV gave it a brand-new rebrand, built the live website at [evolveecoblasting.com](https://www.evolveecoblasting.com/), and did the search work so the right jobs in Edmonton and across the province could actually find it.

According to KR8TIV, the four months that followed brought CAD $1.4 million in enquiries and CAD $450,000 in sales. Todd, Evolve’s founder, went from almost no calls to nonstop calls. Matt knows because he was answering those phones himself, and Todd told him the same thing. Todd has given permission to publish this.

## What was the job?

Dustless blasting sounds like one trade until you listen to the phone. A homeowner with a grey deck, a log-home owner west of Edmonton, a classic-car restorer, a fleet manager, a property manager with graffiti, and a plant that needs specified steel prep are not looking for the same page. They use different words, have different worries, and need different proof.

The public identity and the site had to do three things at once:

* Make it obvious, quickly, which of those buyers Evolve can help.
* Speak to Edmonton and greater Alberta without inventing a local office in every city.
* Give search engines, and people, useful pages for the actual jobs, not a single brochure that tries to mention everything.

Before the work, the phone was quiet. After it, the problem was keeping up. That is the brief, in hindsight: build something a new company could stand on, then make it findable.

## What did we build?

The live site is organised around six kinds of work, each with a path into a page built for that problem: home and hardscape, log homes, automotive and hobbyist work, fleet and heavy equipment, commercial property, and industrial surface preparation. That structure is visible on the homepage today. It is the opposite of a generic “our services” list that hopes the right reader will squint.

Supporting pages include residential, commercial and industrial overviews, plus dedicated pages for log-home restoration, fleet and equipment blasting, and automotive media blasting. There is also a public brand bible. The identity had to work in an industrial setting without looking unapproachable to a homeowner, and the business cards in our [work gallery](/work/) show the same system in print.

On the search side, the important move was to write for real jobs. The Evolve sitemap, checked on 9 November 2026, listed 72 URLs, including 60 blog posts. Those articles are not filler. They are pages about questions such as dustless blasting versus sandblasting, graffiti removal in Edmonton, driveway and log-home restoration, and industrial request-for-quote work. That is a content system a buyer can use, and a search engine can understand.

A back-office site at [ops.evolveecoblasting.com](https://ops.evolveecoblasting.com/) is also live, with an Evolve Ops dashboard login. It is part of the operating layer around the public site.

## The phones, and the numbers

KR8TIV’s figures for the first four months are CAD $1.4 million in enquiries and CAD $450,000 in sales.

The part that is harder to put in a spreadsheet is the sound of the phone. Todd went from almost no calls to nonstop calls. That is not a maybe. Matt was on those calls himself, and Todd told him the change was real.

A new brand and a clear site do not print money on their own. They do make it much easier for a serious buyer in Edmonton or elsewhere in Alberta to find the company, understand the work, and actually get through. In this case, they got through enough that the studio answering the phone could feel it.

## Where it shows up in search

Matt’s account is that Evolve ranked at the top of Google, and in AI answers, ahead of businesses that had been around for years. That claim is about the dustless blasting and eco blasting searches that describe what the company actually does. We are not claiming the same result for every related phrase, and we are not claiming it for “sandblasting Edmonton.”

A separate research check on 9 November 2026, from a web research tool rather than a geotargeted Edmonton Google results page, supports the same pattern on those core queries:

| Query checked | What appeared in the research tool on 9 November 2026 |
|---|---|
| dustless blasting Edmonton | The Evolve homepage was the first result |
| dustless blasting Alberta | The Evolve homepage was the first result |
| eco blasting Alberta | The Evolve homepage was the first result |
| log home restoration Edmonton | The Evolve log-home service page appeared, but not first |
| graffiti removal Edmonton | The Evolve graffiti article appeared, further down a mixed set of results |
| sandblasting Edmonton | Evolve did not appear in the tool’s first five results |

Top for dustless and eco blasting, including the Edmonton query that matters most for local hiring. Mixed, or weaker, on some job-specific phrases and on the more generic sandblasting search. Visibility is query-specific.


## What other Alberta businesses should take from this

Build the site around the different buyers you actually have. Say where you work. Write pages for the jobs people already search for, and keep those pages specific enough to help. Then watch the phone, instead of hoping a pretty homepage is doing the finding for you.
