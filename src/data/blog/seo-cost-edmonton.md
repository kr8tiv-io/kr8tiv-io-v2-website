# How much does SEO cost in Edmonton? Real 2026 prices and what you get

Edmonton agencies that publish their prices charge between about $500 and $3,500 a month for ongoing SEO, with local search packages at the lower end. One-off audits cost a few hundred to around a thousand dollars. Bigger campaigns, for many locations or online stores, cost more.

Those are the agencies brave enough to put prices online. Many do not, which makes it hard for a business owner to know whether a quote is fair. So I collected the published numbers, checked them on 19 November 2026, and added what the work should actually include.

## How do Edmonton prices compare with the wider market?

The best public survey I know of is [Ahrefs' SEO pricing study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/), which polled 439 SEO professionals. It is in US dollars, and the article was last updated on 15 August 2024, so treat it as a rough benchmark. It found:

* The average monthly retainer was **$2,917 US**.
* Local SEO averaged **$1,557 US** a month.
* The most common retainer band was **$501 to $1,000 US** a month, chosen by 20.4 per cent of respondents.
* Agencies averaged **$3,209 US** a month and freelancers **$1,348 US**.
* In the US and Canada subset, **79.1 per cent** charged at least $1,001 a month.
* The most common hourly rate was **$100 to $150 US**.

Edmonton's published prices sit at the low to middle end of that range. That makes sense for a city where most clients are small local businesses, not national brands.

## What should the money actually buy?

"SEO" covers a lot of different work. A monthly fee should be paying for some combination of these, and you should know which:

**Technical fixes.** Making sure Google can crawl and index the site, pages load quickly on a phone, and there are no broken links or duplicate pages. Most of this is front-loaded. It is a lot of work in month one and much less after.

**Google Business Profile.** Categories, services, photos, posts and keeping the details accurate. For a local business this is often the biggest lever. If your profile is missing from Maps, start with [why a Google Business Profile does not show](/blog/google-business-profile-not-showing/).

**Reviews.** A steady, policy-compliant process to ask every customer. [The rules in Canada](/blog/google-reviews-canada-rules/) are stricter than many tools assume.

**Pages and content.** New or improved pages for the services and areas you want more work from, and articles that answer the questions your customers actually ask. I wrote about how that works in [a blog that ranks and brings leads](/blog/blog-that-ranks-and-brings-leads/).

**Local mentions and links.** Being listed accurately in the directories that matter, and earning links from real local sources: suppliers, associations, sponsorships, local news.

**Reporting.** A monthly note on what was done and what changed. Calls, form fills and direction requests matter more than a ranking chart.

A $500 package cannot do all of that every month. That is fine, as long as you know which parts it covers.

## What Google says to watch out for

Google publishes a page for business owners called [Do you need an SEO?](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo). It is worth ten minutes. The parts I would underline:

* **"No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google."** Be wary of anyone who claims to, or who claims a special relationship with Google.
* **Be wary of cold emails** offering SEO. Google says it gets them too.
* **If an SEO creates deceptive content for you, your site could be removed** from Google's index, and you are responsible for what the companies you hire do.
* **You should never have to link to your SEO** from your own site.
* **For an audit, give read access** to Search Console, not write access.

Google also suggests questions to ask, including: can you show examples of past work, do you follow Google Search Essentials, what results do you expect and in what timeframe, how do you measure success, and what is your experience in my city.

## How do I tell whether SEO is working?

Pick numbers that pay the bills:

* Calls and direction requests from your Google Business Profile.
* Form enquiries and quote requests from the website.
* Search Console clicks for the services and areas you care about.
* The number of new reviews each month.

Rankings for a handful of phrases are fine to watch, but they move around, and they differ by where the searcher is standing. A plumber in Mill Woods will not rank the same in Mill Woods as in St. Albert, no matter what anyone charges.

Give it a fair window. Fixing a broken Business Profile or a page Google cannot index can show results within weeks. Moving up for a competitive search usually takes months. If nothing has improved after six months and nobody can explain why, it is reasonable to ask hard questions.

## Can I do it myself?

Yes, and many small businesses should start that way. Google's [SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) is plain-language and covers the basics. The jobs that pay off fastest for an owner are a complete and accurate Business Profile, a habit of asking for reviews, and one clear page for each service you sell. Bring in help when you run out of time, or when the work gets technical.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does SEO cost per month in Edmonton?

Edmonton agencies that publish prices start at about $500 to $750 a month for local SEO, with fuller packages from about $950 to $2,250 and larger campaigns at $2,000 to $3,500 or more. These are their published starting prices in CAD, checked in November 2026.

### Can an SEO company guarantee first place on Google?

No. Google's own guidance says no one can guarantee a number one ranking, and it tells businesses to be wary of any SEO that claims to, or that claims a special relationship with Google.

### How long does SEO take to work?

There is no fixed timeline. Fixes to a Google Business Profile or a broken page can show up within weeks, while ranking for competitive Edmonton searches often takes months of steady work. Ask any provider what results they expect and when, as Google suggests, and get it in writing.

### Is local SEO cheaper than regular SEO?

Usually. In Ahrefs' survey of 439 SEO professionals, local SEO averaged $1,557 US a month against an all-services average of $2,917 US. Edmonton agencies also price local packages at the lower end of their ranges.
