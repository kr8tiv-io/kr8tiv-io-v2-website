# Copy rubric — the critique gate

Every line on this site gets scored against these seven tests. A line
that fails any of them gets rewritten, then re-scored. This is the
critique step of the generate → critique → refine loop; it exists so
"is this good?" becomes a question with an answer rather than a matter
of taste.

1. **Non-duplication.** Does this line already exist, in substance,
   elsewhere on the site? A signature line used four times is not a
   signature, it is a tic.
2. **Specificity.** Does it contain a concrete noun, a number, or a
   claim that could be checked? Or could any agency on earth have
   written it?
3. **Authority.** Does it assert from experience and position, or does
   it hedge and try to please?
4. **Earned edge.** Is the edge doing work, or is it edge for its own
   sake? Edge that reads as flippant costs more than it earns.
5. **Voice.** Founder-direct. Oxford comma. No exclamation points.
   Nothing an account manager would say.
6. **No AI slop.** Banned on sight: elevate, seamless, unlock, leverage,
   journey, "solutions that", "we believe", "in today's landscape",
   "not just X but Y", "passionate about", empty tricolons, and any
   sentence whose only job is to sound like a sentence.
7. **Function.** Does the line differentiate, prove, or move the reader
   toward action? If it were deleted, would anything be lost? If not,
   delete it.
